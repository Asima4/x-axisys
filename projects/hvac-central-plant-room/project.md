A central HVAC plant room serving a large public building in eastern India. Axisys built the coordinated Revit MEP model of the plant room and the terrace cooling tower deck: four chillers, three cooling towers and 22 primary and secondary pumps, with large-bore chilled water and condenser water headers, heating hot water from heat pumps and solar water heaters, domestic, soft and make-up water, drainage, exhaust ductwork, cable trays, lighting and lightning protection. The model was issued for construction, with equipment, pipe, duct and electrical schedules taken straight from it. Open the live model below to explore every element.
